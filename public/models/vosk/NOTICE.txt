en-us-small.tar.gz is the Vosk small US English speech model
(vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15), repackaged unchanged as a tar.gz with the
files in a "model" folder (the format vosk-browser loads).

Source: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models

Used on Android phones by the Physio Chandra virtual assistant to turn the
patient's speech into text on the phone itself (nothing is sent anywhere).
The browser library is vosk-browser (Apache-2.0),
https://github.com/ccoreilly/vosk-browser
