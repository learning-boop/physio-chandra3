## Questions and safety

- S1: "At the inner end of the collarbone, next to the breastbone" (SC joint; AC joint -2).
- S5: one answer (excl). Before, the three slipping answers added up (ceiling 20) and a shoulder slipping with no injury scored 5 of 20.
- S9: the winging tick, with "clearly" to keep it apart from the slight unevenness many people have.
- Injury screen I8 (911): breathing or swallowing trouble, a hoarse voice, or neck or arm swelling after the injury (posterior SC dislocation). Missing from the site before.
- I7 widened to "along your collarbone"; I9: the upper arm's torn-biceps question, asked after a sudden pull from a shoulder-only drawing (same day, the site's tier, not the forms' "within a few days").

## Choices made against the forms (the site's rules win)

- No medication advice in patient text: the forms' anti-inflammatory, pain-relief and injection lines went to clinic notes, or became "ask a pharmacist or your doctor what pain relief is safe for you" (calcific). The frozen shoulder blurb lost its steroid-injection line.
- No "unstable" / "instability" in patient text: the slipping card's subtitle is now "Glenohumeral subluxation or dislocation"; form 6 and 25 questions using "unstable" were not used.
- Tiers: a hot joint stays emergency (every form said same day); a torn biceps stays same day.
- No therapeutic ultrasound for calcific tendinopathy (JOSPT 2025, C), although form 4 lists it.
- Frozen shoulder: "usually one to two years, sometimes longer"; physiotherapy is not said to shorten the course (UK FROST 2020).
- Promises softened (CHCPBC): "helps everyone", "works well", "the treatment with the best evidence", "heal well".
- Every card's non-improvement doctor line: 4 to 6 weeks.

## Weights changed during the checks (not in the reviewers' first draft)

- Strain and sprain: duration +1 (not +2), strain no weight on overhead work and tender spot 1 (not 3); thrower's shoulder onset 1 and no age weights. At the higher weights, one opening answer made them "leaders", and an upper-arm drawing or a neck line lost its own questions (region test "Biceps soreness after the gym", accuracy check neck to shoulder).
- Strain only after a sudden pull or heavy spell, sprain only after a fall or wrench (`onset:` gates); SC joint, strain and sprain from age 5.
- Upper arm: U1 goes early when the drawing stops above the wrist, and U6 (after the gym) has priority; both were crowded out by the new shoulder cards.
- Diabetes lift test patient: no S7 answer (the cuff and frozen shoulder tied at 0.481 vs 0.480 once "reaching behind my back" counted for the cuff).
