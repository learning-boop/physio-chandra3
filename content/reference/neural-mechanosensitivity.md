# Upper-limb neural mechanosensitivity: clinical reasoning supplement

Two separate questions drive the reasoning: (1) is the nerve **sensitive** (gain of function: pain with stretch or pressure) and (2) is it **conducting poorly** (loss of function: numbness, weakness, reflex change)? A patient can have either or both, and the answer changes urgency and treatment. Then: **where** along the nerve is the problem (root, plexus, or a tunnel further down), and is there more than one site?

## A1. Sensitivity versus conduction loss

| Presentation | What the patient notices | What the clinic finds | Engine meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heightened mechanosensitivity (gain of function) | Pain, pulling or tingling in stretch positions; tender along the nerve; normal feeling and strength | Positive neurodynamic test with structural differentiation; nerve trunk tenderness; normal conduction tests | Self-guide suitable; nerve gliding and position changes |
| Conduction loss (loss of function) | Numb patches, weakness, dropping things, muscle thinning; may have little pain | Reduced sensation, strength or reflex in a nerve or root pattern | Book prompt assessment ("nerveLoss" card); progressive → medical referral |
| Mixed | Both of the above | Both findings | Treat sensitivity; monitor conduction closely |
| Widespread / central sensitivity | Symptoms beyond one nerve, both sides, many triggers | Widespread tenderness, sensory changes outside a nerve map | Lower confidence; assessment, broader pain approach |
| Neuropathic pain grading | Possible: history + pain in a plausible neuroanatomical area; Probable: + matching sensory signs; Definite: + confirmatory test | — | Engine reports at most "possible" |

## A2. Upper-limb neurodynamic tests

| Test | Sequence (typical) | Nerve bias | Differentiation / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| ULNT1 | Shoulder girdle depression, abduction ~110°, wrist and finger extension, supination, external rotation, elbow extension | Median nerve, C5-C7 roots | Neck side-bend away increases, towards decreases; wrist release |
| ULNT2a | Girdle depression, elbow extension, external rotation, supination, wrist and finger extension, then abduction | Median nerve | Release depression or wrist |
| ULNT2b | Girdle depression, elbow extension, whole-arm internal rotation, wrist, finger and thumb flexion, ulnar deviation | Radial nerve (superficial radial, PIN) | Release depression; neck side-bend |
| ULNT3 | Wrist and finger extension, pronation, elbow flexion, external rotation, depression, abduction (hand towards the ear) | Ulnar nerve, C8-T1 | Neck side-bend; release wrist |
| Positive test criteria | Reproduces the familiar symptoms at least partly AND a remote movement changes them; side-to-side difference supports | All tests | Stretch sensation alone is a normal response |

## A3. Nerve pathways and where each can be irritated

| Nerve | Sites along the path (neck → hand) | Positions that load it | Symptom area |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brachial plexus | Scalene triangle, between collarbone and first rib, under pectoralis minor | Arms overhead, carrying heavy bags, shoulders slumped forward | Whole arm, often inner arm and ring / little fingers |
| Median | Roots C5-T1; pronator teres and bicipital aponeurosis; carpal tunnel | Wrist bent back or forward for long, gripping, arm out with wrist extended | Thumb, index, middle and half the ring finger (palm side) |
| Ulnar | Lower trunk / thoracic outlet; cubital tunnel; Guyon canal | Elbow held bent (phone, sleeping), leaning on the elbow, cycling handlebars | Ring and little fingers, ulnar side of the hand |
| Radial | Spiral groove; radial tunnel below the outer elbow (PIN); superficial branch at the thumb-side wrist | Arm draped over a chair, repeated forearm rotation, tight watch strap or cuffs | Back of forearm, back of thumb and first web space; PIN = weakness only |
| Musculocutaneous | Coracobrachialis; lateral forearm cutaneous branch at the elbow crease | Heavy lifting with the elbow bent, carrying | Outer forearm |

## A4. Local tests at common entrapment sites

| Site | Tests | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carpal tunnel | Wainner rule: shaking relieves, wrist ratio >0.67, symptom severity score >1.9, reduced median thumb sensation, age >45; plus Tinel, Phalen | All 5 positive: +LR about 18 (Wainner 2005) | Single tests alone have modest accuracy |
| Cubital tunnel | Elbow flexion test (± pressure), Tinel at the ulnar groove, Froment | Moderate; combine with history | Check the inner forearm to exclude C8 / plexus |
| Radial tunnel / PIN | Tenderness 4-5 cm below the lateral epicondyle; resisted supination; finger drop in PIN | Limited data | Often confused with tennis elbow |
| Thoracic outlet | Elevated arm stress test, Roos, costoclavicular manoeuvre | Low specificity; many false positives | Reproduce symptoms, do not diagnose alone |
| Cervical root | Wainner cervical cluster (content/reference/cervical-radiculopathy.md) | 3 of 4 positive: +LR 6.1 | Always screen the neck |

## A5. Root, plexus or peripheral nerve?

| Finding | Points towards | Engine action |
|---|---|---|
| Symptoms change with neck movement; dermatomal pattern; reflex change | Nerve root (neck) | Add radiculopathy (N3 scores both) |
| Inner forearm numb with ring / little finger symptoms | C8 root, lower trunk or thoracic outlet (not ulnar nerve) | Neck / plexus, not cubital tunnel |
| Thumb-base palm feeling normal with thumb to middle finger tingling | Median nerve at the carpal tunnel | Favour carpal tunnel |
| Symptoms only with arms overhead or carrying, whole arm | Brachial plexus / thoracic outlet | Assessment (base of the neck) |
| Weakness without numbness in finger and thumb extension | Posterior interosseous nerve | Prompt assessment |
| Findings at two levels (neck and wrist, or neck and elbow) | Double crush (Upton and McComas 1973) | Keep both; do not force one answer |

## A6. Weighting rules for the pattern engine

| Pattern the patient reports | Reasoning | Weight / route | Where it is built |
|---|---|---|---|
| Symptoms along a nerve line, brought on by stretch positions, eased by unloading | Classic mechanosensitivity | Raise this record | N14 |
| Same, plus neck pain that changes the arm symptoms | Neck as source or interface | Show this record and radiculopathy | N3 scores both |
| Night tingling in thumb to middle fingers, shaking helps | Median at the wrist | Favour carpal tunnel | Wrist and hand areas |
| Stretch sensation only, no reproduction of usual symptoms | Normal neural response | Do not raise this record | Wording of N14 ("bring them on") |
| Persistent numbness, weakness or wasting | Conduction loss | Prompt booking; progressive → GP | N14 "loss" card; nrf-myelo |
| Both hands, or hands and feet | Systemic neuropathy or spinal cord | Stop scoring → screen | N9 (-16); nrf-cord |
| Colour, temperature or swelling changes | Vascular or CRPS | Red-flag screen | Base of the neck and upper arm flags |

## B. Scope boundaries

- **In scope:** one-sided arm symptoms from heightened mechanosensitivity of the brachial plexus or the median, ulnar or radial nerves, with or without mild peripheral entrapment and without progressive loss of function.
- **Routed elsewhere:** neck-led dermatomal pattern → radiculopathy; established carpal or cubital tunnel → their own records; progressive weakness, wasting, bilateral or systemic symptoms, vascular signs → red-flag screen or GP; post-surgical or post-fracture nerve injury → assessment.

## A6. Patient self-tests (added 6 Oct 2026)

Butler's active quick tests (NOI neurodynamic techniques workbook), offered online as the neck's optional N15 to a stretch-sensitive picture without conduction loss or cord signs:

| Self-test | Nerve bias | Confirm in clinic |
|---|---|---|
| Arm out at shoulder height, palm up, wrist back, then head tilt away | Median, C5-C7 | ULNT1 / ULNT2a with differentiation |
| Hand flat over the ear, then lift the elbow up and out | Ulnar, C8-T1 | ULNT3; cubital tunnel, Guyon canal |
| Arm by the side, fist round the thumb, elbow straight, arm turned in, shoulder down | Radial (superficial radial, PIN) | ULNT2b; radial tunnel vs lateral elbow |

A home test is not a clinician's structural differentiation: it suggests a sensitive nerve and which one, to be repeated and compared side to side. Not scored (the condition's ceiling stays 12); a negative set counts -2 against it.
