- **RED-S / athlete amenorrhoea with stress fractures** (AIM p. 623): the stress-fracture questions and STRESS_LINE cover the bone side.

- **Hyperprolactinaemia and hypogonadism** (bone loss): in the osteopenia risk question ("low testosterone, hormone treatment").

- **Scheuermann's disease** (Word document; AIM pp. 582-583): a teen upper-back card (Sorensen criteria).
