## Safety changes

- ft-septic (emergency): a toe joint or the foot hot, red and swollen with a fever or feeling unwell. The foot had no infected-joint question (the ankle has af-septic).
- ft-gout and af-gout (same group): now same day.
- lgf-stressday and ft-stressday (same day): a shin bone that hurts with every step, at rest, or too much to stand on; a foot you cannot take weight on, or a foot bone that hurts at rest. As the hip's hpf-stressday; for the shin this is stricter than form 5 ("book urgently, crutches"). lgf-stress and ft-stress name the risks (missed periods, low bone density, under-eating, a past stress fracture) and the high-risk sites.
- af-locked (same day): an ankle stuck right now, in a new group "Signs from inside the ankle joint" (the shared joint theme, now titled "Signs from inside the joint").
- Thigh injury screen: I7 cannot straighten the knee or lift the straight leg (torn quadriceps tendon); I8 blood thinners or a bleeding disorder with a large bruise or swelling. Both same day.
- ankle-atfl: a sore spot below the tip of the outer ankle bone, deep at the back, or on the outer foot in front of the ankle, not easing 1 to 2 weeks after a sprain: see the doctor (lateral or posterior talar process, anterior calcaneal process fractures).

## A correction to the forms

The Achilles forms (3, 6, 11, 12) quote the Thompson test as "Sn 29–76%, Sp 96–100%, +LR 3.3–43.1, −LR 0.3–0.8 (Table 7-4)". Those are the knee manual's quadriceps active test figures (PCL, Table 6-7). The ankle manual's Table 7-4 cites Schwieterman 2013 using Maffulli 1998's data (n=161), about 96% sensitive and 93% specific. The card notes carry the corrected figures.
