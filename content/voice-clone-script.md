# Recording your voice for the clone

Read this aloud once, calmly, the way you would talk to a patient in the
clinic. It takes about two minutes. It covers the sounds the assistant uses
(questions, numbers, "yes" and "no", body parts), so the clone learns how you
say them.

## Before you press record

- A quiet room with soft things around (a bedroom or a car works well). No
  fan, fridge hum, music or echo.
- Phone voice recorder is fine. Hold it about a hand's width (15–20 cm) from
  your mouth, a little to the side, and keep that distance.
- Speak at your normal, warm pace. Don't read like an announcer.
- If you stumble, pause and carry on from the start of the sentence; small
  pauses are fine. One take of 1½ to 3 minutes is ideal.
- Save it as M4A, MP3 or WAV.

## What to read

Hey there, and welcome! I'm the virtual assistant of Physio Chandra, and it's
great to have you here. Chandra is a registered physiotherapist in Surrey and
Burnaby.

This guide helps you describe your pain before your visit. You mark where it
hurts on a 3D body, and then answer a few short questions. It takes about five
to fifteen minutes. Take your time, eh. There are no wrong answers.

Let me show you how. To turn the body, swipe across it with one finger, like
this. Then tap Draw, and trace over every place that hurts. If you make a
mistake, no worries, just tap Undo.

How did the knee pain start? Was it gradual, or after a fall or a twist? How
long has it been going on: less than two weeks, two to six weeks, or more than
three months? At its worst, how bad is the pain, from one to ten?

You're thirty to forty-nine. Is that right? Great. You answered: sharp pain
when you move. Is that right? Okay. Thanks. Perfect. Got it.

Does your shoulder click, catch or lock? Do you notice any numbness, tingling
or pins and needles in your hand, wrist or fingers? Is there any swelling in
your ankle, foot or lower back?

Sorry, I missed that one. Mind saying it again? You can say your answer, or tap
it. Yes. No. Next. Go back. That's all.

If this is happening now, please call nine-one-one or go to the nearest
emergency department. For advice that is not urgent, you can call HealthLink
B.C. at eight-one-one.

At the end, you'll see general information, not a diagnosis, and you can book
an assessment with Chandra if you'd like. Thanks for taking the time. See you
soon!
