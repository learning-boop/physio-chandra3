## blurb
A strain of the inner-thigh muscles that control side-to-side movement — the classic sports groin injury from sprinting, kicking, or a sudden change of direction.

## noticed
- Sudden inner-thigh/groin pain during sport
- Pain squeezing the knees together or side-lunging
- Tenderness along the inner thigh
- Bruising in larger strains

## homeCare
- Relative rest from sprinting/kicking early on — keep walking as comfortable
- Early gentle range, then progressive adductor strengthening (e.g., ball squeezes)
- Return to sport gradually via straight-line running before cutting

## seePhysioIf
- Pain limits walking beyond a few days
- You want a criteria-based return-to-sport plan — re-injury is common without one
- Groin pain keeps recurring each season
