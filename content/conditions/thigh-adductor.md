## blurb
The inner thigh muscles pull the legs together and steady the hip when you change direction. A sudden kick, a side-step, or a slip into the splits can strain them, giving pain along the inner thigh that is worse squeezing the knees together or pushing off to the side.

## noticed
- A sudden pain along the inner thigh during sport or a slip
- Pain squeezing the knees together or side-stepping
- Tenderness, and sometimes bruising, on the inner thigh

## homeCare
- Ease off kicking, side-stepping, and wide stretches for a while
- Keep walking as comfort allows
- Start gentle squeezing exercises (a ball between the knees) as it settles

## seePhysioIf
- Walking is still painful after a few days
- The pain is moving up into the groin
- You want a plan to return to sport
- A pop with a large bruise on the inner thigh, or you cannot walk properly: see a doctor the same day
- A lump in the groin that appears when you cough or stand: see your doctor
- A deep groin or inner-thigh ache that is worse with every run or hop, or at night: see a doctor before more running

## physioHelps
<!-- Approved by Chandra Matla, 17 Oct 2026 (review: content/review/physioHelps-review-2026-10-17.docx) -->
- A physiotherapist can check your inner thigh and explain how bad the strain looks.
- Squeezing exercises and step-by-step strengthening can help the muscle heal and get stronger.
- You get a gradual plan to get back to running, kicking and side-to-side sport.
