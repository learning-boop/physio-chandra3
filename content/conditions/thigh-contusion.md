## blurb
A hard knock to the thigh, such as a knee in football or a tackle, bruises the muscle underneath: the "dead leg". The thigh is sore, swollen, and stiff, and how far the knee will bend is a good guide to how bad the bruise is. Gentle movement early, and not massaging or heating it in the first days, helps it settle.

## noticed
- Pain, swelling, and bruising where the thigh was hit
- The knee is stiff and will not bend fully
- Limping or pain when running

## homeCare
- In the first day, rest it with the knee gently bent, and use a cold pack
- Avoid massage, heat, and alcohol in the first few days: they can increase bleeding in the muscle
- Start gentle knee bending as soon as it is comfortable

## seePhysioIf
- The knee still will not bend past halfway after a few days
- You feel a hard lump in the muscle weeks later
- You want a plan to return to sport
