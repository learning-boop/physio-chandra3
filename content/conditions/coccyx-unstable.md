## blurb
The tailbone normally tilts slightly as you sit down and stand up. After childbirth, or with changes in weight, it can move more than it should, so it catches as you move between sitting and standing. The typical sign is a sharp pain at the moment you stand up.

## noticed
- A sharp pain at the tailbone as you get up from sitting
- Sitting on hard seats hurts
- It started after giving birth, often a long or assisted delivery

## homeCare
- Use a wedge or cut-out cushion, and lean forward slightly when you sit
- Shift your weight forward onto your thighs before standing up
- Break up long spells of sitting
- Soften stools so you do not strain

## seePhysioIf
- It hasn't eased after 2 months
- Standing up from sitting keeps catching
- You want an assessment; treatment of the tailbone and the muscles around it helps many people
