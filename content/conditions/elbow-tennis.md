## blurb
An overload of the tendons that lift the wrist and fingers, felt at the outer elbow — despite the name, most cases come from gripping work, desk work, or DIY rather than tennis.

## noticed
- Pain or burning at the outer elbow with wrist movements
- Weakened grip — a full cup or a handshake hurts
- Tenderness on the outer bony bump
- Worse with lifting, gripping, twisting

## homeCare
- Temporarily reduce the most aggravating grip loads — don't stop using the arm entirely
- Lift with the palm up where possible
- Gradual strengthening of the forearm is the proven path — tendons adapt to progressive load

## seePhysioIf
- Pain persists beyond 2–3 weeks despite self-care
- Daily tasks like holding a cup are limited
- Strength is gradually dropping — a graded loading program is the evidence-based treatment
