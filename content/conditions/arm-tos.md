## blurb
The nerves to the arm pass through a narrow gap between the collarbone, the first rib and the muscles at the side of the neck. When the first rib sits high or those muscles are tight, the nerves can be irritated, giving tingling and heaviness down the inner arm, usually with the arms raised or loaded.

## noticed
- Tingling or numbness in the ring and little fingers or along the inner forearm
- The arm feels heavy or tires quickly, especially with the arms overhead or carrying bags
- An ache above the collarbone at the base of the neck on one side

## homeCare
- Take regular breaks from overhead work and carry loads close to the body, split between both hands
- Use a backpack with both straps rather than a shoulder bag
- Support your arms on armrests or a desk when you sit, so the shoulders are not held up

## seePhysioIf
- Tingling or heaviness keeps coming back with overhead work or carrying
- It is affecting your grip, work or sleep
- You want a plan for posture, breathing and first-rib mobility that eases the outlet
- Your whole arm suddenly becomes swollen, heavy or bluish: see a doctor the same day; call 911 if you are also short of breath or have chest pain
- A hand or fingers suddenly become cold, pale or numb: go to the emergency department now

## physioHelps
<!-- Approved by Chandra Matla, 17 Oct 2026 (review: content/review/physioHelps-review-2026-10-17.docx) -->
- A physiotherapist can check your neck, shoulders and posture and explain what irritates the nerves.
- Exercises for the shoulder blade muscles and tight neck muscles can help calm the symptoms.
- You get advice on overhead work, carrying and your desk set-up to help keep symptoms settled.
