## blurb
A few finger-widths above the wrist on the thumb side, two sets of tendons that move the thumb and wrist cross over each other. A sudden increase in repeated wrist movement, such as rowing, paddling, weights, or a new job, can make them rub and swell where they cross, sometimes with a squeaking or creaking feel.

## noticed
- Pain and swelling on the top of the forearm, above the wrist on the thumb side
- A squeaking or creaking feeling there when the wrist moves
- It started after a new or bigger amount of repeated wrist work

## homeCare
- Cut back the repeated wrist movement that brought it on for a week or two
- Keep the wrist and thumb moving gently within comfort
- A cold pack over the sore spot can ease the swelling
- Build the activity back up gradually rather than all at once

## seePhysioIf
- It has not settled after 2 weeks of easing off
- The swelling or creaking keeps coming back when you restart
- You want a plan to return to rowing, paddling, lifting, or work
