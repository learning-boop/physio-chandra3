## blurb
At the front of the ankle, the shin bone and the ankle bone can pinch soft tissue or small bony spurs between them when the ankle bends right up, as in a deep squat, a lunge, or going down stairs. It is common after old sprains and in footballers and dancers. The pain is a pinch in the front crease rather than an ache around the whole ankle.

## noticed
- A pinching pain in the front crease of the ankle
- Worse squatting, lunging, going downhill or downstairs
- Often after old sprains or a lot of kicking or jumping

## homeCare
- Avoid forcing the ankle into deep bends for a while
- A small heel raise in the shoe can ease it
- Keep the ankle moving in the range that does not pinch

## seePhysioIf
- The pinching keeps coming back
- It is limiting squatting, running, or sport
- The ankle also swells or catches
