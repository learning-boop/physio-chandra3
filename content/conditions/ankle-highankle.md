## blurb
Just above the ankle, the two bones of the lower leg are held together by strong ligaments. A twist with the foot planted and turning outwards, common in football and rugby, can sprain them. The pain sits higher than an ordinary sprain, at the front between the two bones, and it often takes longer to recover. Some need a surgeon's opinion, so it is worth having it assessed.

## noticed
- Pain at the front of the ankle, just above the joint, between the two leg bones
- Worse pushing off, twisting, or going up on the toes
- Slower to settle than an ordinary rolled ankle

## homeCare
- Protect it early: walk only as comfort allows, with a boot or crutches if needed
- Avoid twisting and pushing off hard while it settles
- Keep the rest of the leg moving and strong

## seePhysioIf
- It has not improved within 1 to 2 weeks
- It is limiting walking or sport
- You need a graded plan back to twisting sport
