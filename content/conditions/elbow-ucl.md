## blurb
The ligament on the inner side of the elbow takes a large strain each time the arm is cocked back to throw. Repeated throwing, or one hard throw, can strain it, giving pain on the inside of the elbow as the arm goes back and a loss of speed or accuracy. A pop on the inside of the elbow needs to be checked promptly.

## noticed
- Pain on the inner elbow as the arm goes back to throw
- Losing throwing speed, distance, or accuracy
- Sometimes tingling into the little finger when throwing

## homeCare
- Rest from throwing while it is painful; keep the rest of the body training
- Keep the elbow moving gently
- Return to throwing gradually, with fewer and softer throws at first

## seePhysioIf
- Throwing still hurts after 2 weeks of rest
- You felt a pop, or the elbow feels unstable
- You want a graded throwing programme to return to sport
