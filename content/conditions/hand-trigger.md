## blurb
The tendons that bend the fingers run through tight bands in the palm. If a tendon thickens or the band tightens, the finger catches or clicks as it bends, and can lock bent. The tender spot is in the palm at the base of the finger, although many people feel it at the middle finger joint. It is more common with diabetes and heavy gripping.

## noticed
- A finger or thumb that clicks, catches, or locks bent, often worse in the morning
- A tender lump in the palm at the base of that finger
- Pain at the middle joint of the finger when it catches

## homeCare
- Ease off heavy or repeated gripping for a while
- Gently move the finger through its range; do not force a locked finger straight
- Use padded or thicker handles on tools

## seePhysioIf
- The finger catches often or locks and has to be freed
- It has not settled after a few weeks of easing off
- You want advice on splinting and exercises
