## blurb
Each finger has two small nerves running down its sides. Pressure from gripping a tool, a bowling ball, or scissors, or a knock or cut, can irritate one of them, giving numbness or tingling down one side of one finger only. Nerve problems higher up affect more than one side or more than one finger.

## noticed
- Numbness or tingling down one side of one finger
- Worse after gripping tools, scissors, or a bowling ball
- Sometimes a tender spot where the nerve is pressed

## homeCare
- Pad or change the grip of the tool or ball that presses on the finger
- Avoid pressure on the tender spot while it settles
- Keep the finger moving normally

## seePhysioIf
- The numbness has not eased after a few weeks of taking the pressure off
- It followed a cut: that needs a doctor promptly
- The numbness is spreading to other fingers
