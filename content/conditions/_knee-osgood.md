## blurb
This is irritation of the growing bone just below the kneecap, common in active kids and teens who are going through a growth spurt.

## noticed
- Tenderness, and sometimes a small bony bump, just below the kneecap
- Pain that's worse during or after running, jumping, or kneeling
- Usually seen in children or teenagers who play a lot of sport

## homeCare
- Cut back on jumping and running for a while to let it settle
- Icing the tender spot after activity can sometimes help
- Gentle stretching of the front and back of the thigh may ease tension

## seePhysioIf
- The pain isn't settling after a few weeks of reduced activity
- It's getting in the way of sport or everyday activity
