## blurb
At the point of the elbow there is a small fluid sac (bursa) that cushions the bone, and the triceps tendon attaches just above it. Leaning on the elbow, or a knock, can irritate the bursa so that it fills with fluid into a soft swelling; heavy pushing can overload the tendon. A red, hot swelling or one with a cut over it needs a doctor, as it may be infected.

## noticed
- A soft, sometimes squashy swelling at the point of the elbow
- Tender to lean on
- Pain at the back of the elbow with pushing or straightening against resistance

## homeCare
- Avoid leaning on the elbow; pad desks and armrests
- Protect it from knocks while it settles
- Ease off heavy pushing, push-ups, or dips for a while

## seePhysioIf
- The swelling or pain has not settled after 2 to 3 weeks
- It keeps coming back
- Pushing or straightening the arm is getting weaker
