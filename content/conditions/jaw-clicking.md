## blurb
Inside the jaw joint, a small disc of cartilage cushions the jaw as it moves. If the disc sits slightly forward, it can slip back into place with a click as you open or close. A clicking jaw is very common and often painless; when it hurts, it usually settles with the right jaw habits.

## noticed
- A click or pop just in front of the ear when opening or closing
- The jaw may swing slightly to one side as it opens
- Chewing hard food or yawning wide can make the joint ache

## homeCare
- Choose softer food for a while and cut it into small pieces
- Avoid opening very wide: support your chin when you yawn
- Rest position: lips together, teeth apart, tongue resting on the roof of the mouth
- Stop gum chewing and nail or pen biting

## seePhysioIf
- The jaw starts to catch or lock
- Pain lasts more than a few weeks or limits eating
- You want exercises to control how the jaw opens
