## blurb
The small joints at the back of the spine can become irritated, typically causing one-sided back pain that dislikes arching backwards or long standing.

## noticed
- One-sided low back ache
- Worse arching back, standing long, or looking up overhead
- Usually eases when sitting or bending slightly forward

## homeCare
- Gentle knees-to-chest and pelvic tilt movements
- Break up long standing with brief sitting or a step stool
- Heat for comfort
- Short regular walks

## seePhysioIf
- It lingers beyond ~2 weeks
- It recurs with particular activities
- You'd like specific mobility and strength work for it
