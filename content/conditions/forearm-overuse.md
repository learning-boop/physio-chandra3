## blurb
The muscles that grip and move the wrist and fingers fill most of the forearm. Long spells of gripping, typing, or tool use, or a jump in how much you do, can leave them aching and tired across the forearm. There is no nerve tingling, and it settles as the load is spread out and built up gradually.

## noticed
- A spread-out ache across the forearm muscles
- Worse after long spells of gripping, typing, or tool use
- Tender, tired muscles, with no tingling in the fingers

## homeCare
- Break up long gripping or typing sessions with short pauses
- Check your workstation, grip size, and tool set-up
- Gentle forearm stretches and heat can ease the ache
- Build heavier or longer tasks back up gradually

## seePhysioIf
- The ache has not settled after 2 to 3 weeks of changes
- It is limiting your work or sport
- You start to notice tingling or weakness in the hand
