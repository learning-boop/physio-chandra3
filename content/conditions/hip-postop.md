## blurb
After a hip replacement or a hip fracture operation, a guided programme of exercise, walking and balance practice helps you get stronger, steadier and back to everyday activities, and lowers the chance of a fall. Most people keep improving for many months, and it is never too late to build strength.

## noticed
- Stiffness and weakness around the operated hip, and tiring quickly when walking
- Difficulty with stairs, getting up from a chair, putting on socks or getting in and out of a car
- Feeling less steady on your feet, or worried about falling

## homeCare
- Follow your surgeon's advice on movement limits and weight bearing, for as long as you were told
- Walk a little and often, building up the distance; use your walking aid until you are steady without it
- Do your strengthening and balance exercises most days
- Make your home safer: clear walkways, good lighting, a raised toilet seat or grab rails if needed

## seePhysioIf
- Sudden severe hip pain, a clunk, you cannot stand on the leg, or the leg looks shorter or turned: call 911
- The hip is newly warm, red or swollen, the wound is leaking, or you have a fever: see a doctor the same day
- A swollen, warm or tender calf or thigh: see a doctor the same day; if you are also short of breath or have chest pain, call 911
- You would like a structured strength, walking and balance programme, or have had a fall
- Progress has stalled, or you are not back to the activities you want

## clinicNotes
- Hip fracture (JOSPT CPG 2021): post-acute rehabilitation with progressive strength (including weight-bearing) and balance training (A), activity training and a home programme; screen fall risk, cognition, nutrition and bone health (refer for osteoporosis management); measures: TUG, gait speed, 30-s chair stand, SPPB, balance.
- Hip replacement: follow the surgeon's precautions (approach-specific); progressive strengthening, gait and functional training; outcome HOOS or Oxford Hip Score.
- Safety: dislocation (hpf-dislocation, 911), infection or loosening (hpf-replacement, same day), DVT or PE (hpf-dvt, 911 if breathless), new fracture after a fall (the injury screen).
- Sources: JOSPT hip fracture CPG 2021 (McDonough et al.); Koc TA et al., JOSPT 2025 (hip OA); content/reference/hip-fracture.md.
