## blurb
This is irritation right where the tendon attaches to the bottom tip of the kneecap, most often seen in growing children and young teens who do a lot of jumping or running.

## noticed
- Tenderness right at the lower tip of the kneecap
- Pain that builds up with jumping, running, or kneeling
- Most common in active children and younger teenagers

## homeCare
- Reduce jumping and sprinting activity for a while
- Icing after activity can help if it feels sore
- Gentle thigh stretching and a gradual return to sport

## seePhysioIf
- Symptoms stick around despite backing off activity
- It's limiting participation in sport or school activities
