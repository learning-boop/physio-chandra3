## blurb
The joints where the stiff ribcage spine meets the mobile low back take a lot of twisting. After a lift, twist or long spells of sitting they can stiffen and ache on one side at the bottom of the ribs, easing as you get moving.

## noticed
- A sore spot beside the spine at the bottom of the ribs
- Twisting, or straightening up after bending, catches it
- Stiff at first, easing as you move

## homeCare
- Keep moving: short walks and gentle trunk turns through the day
- Break up long spells of sitting or driving
- Heat, and lying on your back with the knees bent, to ease a flare

## seePhysioIf
- It hasn't settled after 2 weeks
- It keeps coming back with lifting or sport
- You'd like a mobility and strength plan for the junction
