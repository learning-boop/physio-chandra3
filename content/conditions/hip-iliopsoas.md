## blurb
The hip flexor muscle runs down the front of the hip into the top of the thigh. Running, kicking, or a lot of repeated hip lifting can overload it, giving pain at the front of the hip when you lift your knee. Some people also feel or hear it snap over the front of the hip.

## noticed
- Pain at the front of the hip or groin when lifting the knee
- Worse with kicking, sprinting, or climbing
- Sometimes a snap or clunk at the front of the hip

## homeCare
- Ease off kicking, sprinting, and hill work for a while
- Gentle hip-flexor stretches within comfort
- Build back gradually with strengthening before returning to sport

## seePhysioIf
- It has not settled after 2 to 3 weeks
- The snapping is painful or getting more frequent
- You want a plan to return to running or kicking
