## blurb
Stiffness and muscle ache of the mid-back region, commonly linked to sustained postures — the upper back tolerates load well but dislikes staying still.

## noticed
- Ache between the shoulder blades after sitting
- Stiffness with rotation or looking behind
- Eases with movement and activity

## homeCare
- Break up sitting regularly; vary positions
- Gentle extension over a chair back and rotation stretches
- Stay generally active — walking helps the whole spine

## seePhysioIf
- Stiffness or ache persists beyond 2 weeks
- It limits work, sport, or sleep
- You'd like a posture-and-strength plan for desk work
