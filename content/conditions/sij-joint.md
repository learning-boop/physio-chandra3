## blurb
The joint between the base of the spine and the pelvis can become sensitive — often after pregnancy, a fall onto the buttock, or an uneven load — causing focal pain near the pelvic dimple.

## noticed
- Pointable pain near the dimple at the back of the pelvis
- Worse rolling in bed, standing on one leg, or climbing stairs
- May spread into the buttock or groin

## homeCare
- Avoid prolonged single-leg loading while irritable
- A support belt can help short-term in some cases
- Gentle gluteal and core activation within comfort
- Even, symmetrical standing habits

## seePhysioIf
- Pain persists more than ~2 weeks
- It began after pregnancy or a fall
- Walking or stairs remain limited
