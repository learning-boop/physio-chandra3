## blurb
A firm knot of tissue can form within the arch of the foot, sometimes causing tenderness when you press on it or wear certain shoes.

## noticed
- A noticeable, firm lump along the arch of the foot
- Tenderness when pressing on the lump or standing on it
- Long-standing discomfort that doesn't follow the usual 'worse in the morning' pattern

## homeCare
- Wear shoes with extra cushioning under the arch to take pressure off the area
- Avoid tight or narrow shoes that press directly on the lump
- Gentle arch stretching if it doesn't increase discomfort

## seePhysioIf
- You notice a lump that seems to be growing or becoming more painful
- Discomfort continues despite footwear changes and rest
