## blurb
A tendon runs in a groove on the back of the wrist, on the little-finger side. Repeated turning of the forearm, or a racquet or golf swing, can irritate it or let it flick in and out of its groove, giving pain and sometimes a visible snap when you turn your palm up and down.

## noticed
- Pain on the back of the wrist, little-finger side
- A snapping or flicking feeling when you turn the palm up and down
- Worse with racquet or golf swings, or repeated twisting

## homeCare
- Ease off repeated forceful turning of the forearm for a while
- Avoid the grip or swing that makes it snap
- Keep the wrist and fingers moving gently within comfort

## seePhysioIf
- It has not settled after 2 to 3 weeks
- The tendon keeps snapping out of place
- You want a plan to return to racquet sports, golf, or tool work
