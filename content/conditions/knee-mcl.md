## blurb
This is a stretch or tear of the ligament running along the inner side of your knee, usually caused by a sideways force or an awkward twist.

## noticed
- Pain right along the inner side of the knee after a blow or twisting injury
- Tenderness when pressing directly over the inner knee
- A feeling of the knee giving slightly when it's pushed inward

## homeCare
- Rest from activities that stress the inner knee and use ice to settle early pain and swelling
- Consider a supportive brace if it helps you feel steadier, based on your own comfort
- Slowly reintroduce bending, straightening, and light strengthening as pain allows

## seePhysioIf
- Pain, swelling, or a wobbly feeling hasn't improved within a couple of weeks
- The knee feels unstable or buckles during everyday walking or turning
