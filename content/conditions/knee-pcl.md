## blurb
This ligament sits deep at the back of the knee and can be strained by a hard blow to the front of the shin or a heavy fall onto a bent knee.

## noticed
- A deep ache at the back of the knee, especially when kneeling or slowing down quickly
- The injury often followed a direct hit to the shin or a fall onto a bent knee
- A sense that the lower leg sits slightly further back than usual

## homeCare
- Avoid kneeling or twisting on the knee while it recovers
- Use ice and gentle elevation to manage early swelling
- A supportive brace can be considered while the ligament heals, based on comfort

## seePhysioIf
- Deep knee pain or swelling continues beyond a couple of weeks
- The knee feels like it shifts or gives way during normal daily movement
