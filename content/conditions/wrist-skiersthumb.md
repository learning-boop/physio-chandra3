## blurb
This is a sprain or tear of the ligament that supports the base of the thumb, often from a fall, a ski pole, or catching a ball awkwardly.

## noticed
- Pain and swelling on the inner side of the thumb near the knuckle
- The thumb feels wobbly or weak when pinching or holding objects
- Discomfort with gripping, throwing, or catching activities

## homeCare
- Rest the thumb and avoid forceful pinching or gripping for a few days
- Support the thumb with tape or a simple splint if it feels unstable
- Apply ice to ease pain and swelling in the first couple of days

## seePhysioIf
- The thumb feels loose, unstable, or gives way with normal use
- Pain and swelling haven't started easing within one to two weeks
