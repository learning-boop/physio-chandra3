## blurb
Ongoing back pain can sometimes be kept going by worry, stress, or fear of movement rather than by tissue damage alone. Addressing these feelings alongside your body often helps recovery move faster.

## noticed
- Worrying that movement or activity will cause more damage to your back
- Feeling low, discouraged, or overly focused on the pain
- Avoiding normal activities out of fear, even when your body seems ready for them

## homeCare
- Stay as active as you comfortably can rather than resting for long stretches
- Remind yourself that most low back pain settles well with time and movement
- Use pacing — gradually building back up to normal activities step by step

## seePhysioIf
- Fear of movement or low mood is stopping you from returning to normal activities
- Your pain has lasted several weeks and isn't improving with general activity
