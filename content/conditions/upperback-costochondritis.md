## blurb
The joints where the ribs meet the breastbone can become irritated, often after a chest infection, a bout of coughing, or new pushing exercise. It is sore to press and usually settles over weeks. A first episode of chest pain should still be checked by a doctor.

## noticed
- Tenderness when pressing on the breastbone or where the ribs join it
- Worse with pushing, hugging, or lying on the front
- Often starts after a cough or chest infection

## homeCare
- Ease off pushing exercises and heavy carrying for now
- A heat pack over the front of the chest
- Gentle chest stretches in a doorway, within comfort

## seePhysioIf
- It has not eased after a few weeks
- It keeps coming back
- You want a plan to return to the gym or sport
