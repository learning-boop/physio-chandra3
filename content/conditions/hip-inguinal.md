## blurb
Just above the groin crease is a weaker area of the tummy wall, where the inguinal canal runs. In sports with a lot of kicking, sprinting, and twisting, this area can become painful, especially with coughing, sneezing, or sit-ups. A doctor should check for a hernia if there is a lump.

## noticed
- Pain just above the groin crease
- Worse with coughing, sneezing, sit-ups, or sprinting
- No visible lump (a lump needs a doctor to check for a hernia)

## homeCare
- Ease off sprinting, kicking, and sit-ups for a while
- Keep active with straight-line walking or cycling if comfortable
- Build core and hip strength back gradually

## seePhysioIf
- It keeps limiting your sport after 2 to 3 weeks
- It keeps coming back each season
- You notice a lump: see a doctor first
