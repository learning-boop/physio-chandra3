## blurb
A nerve that runs through the muscles at the top of the forearm can be squeezed where it passes a few finger-widths below the outer elbow. It gives a deep, tiring ache in the forearm rather than pain on the bony bump, and usually no numbness. It often sits alongside tennis elbow and is easy to mistake for it.

## noticed
- A deep ache in the top of the forearm, below the outer elbow
- Worse with twisting the forearm, turning a screwdriver, or repeated gripping
- Tiredness in the forearm, often without tingling or numbness

## homeCare
- Ease off the repeated twisting and gripping that bring it on
- Take short breaks and change hand position during tool or computer work
- Keep the arm moving gently rather than resting it completely

## seePhysioIf
- The ache has not eased after 2 to 3 weeks
- Tennis elbow treatment has not helped
- You notice weakness lifting the fingers or the wrist
