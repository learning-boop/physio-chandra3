## blurb
Sometimes a disc in the low back can irritate nearby tissues, causing pain that spreads from the low back into the buttock or thigh, especially with bending or sitting.

## noticed
- Pain that's worse with bending forward, sitting, or staying slouched for a while
- Pain that can shift and settle closer to the spine with certain positions or movements
- Possibly a slight lean to one side or difficulty straightening up fully

## homeCare
- Try gentle backward-bending movements or positions that ease pain back toward the low back rather than the leg
- Avoid long periods of sitting or slouched posture for the first couple of weeks
- Stay generally active with short, frequent walks rather than complete rest

## seePhysioIf
- Bending or sitting consistently brings on or worsens pain into your thigh or leg
- Your pain isn't easing with simple position changes after a couple of weeks
