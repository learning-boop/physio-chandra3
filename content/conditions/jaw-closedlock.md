## blurb
When the cartilage disc in the jaw joint slips forward and stays there, it can block the jaw from opening fully. Often a jaw that used to click suddenly stops clicking and will not open wide. It is worth having assessed early, because gentle, guided movement helps most when it starts soon.

## noticed
- A jaw that used to click, then stopped, and now will not open fully
- Opening less than three fingers wide
- The jaw may swing towards the sore side as it opens

## homeCare
- Soft food, small bites, and avoid forcing the jaw open
- Warm packs on the cheek and gentle, pain-free opening and closing
- Lips together, teeth apart during the day

## seePhysioIf
- Book soon: early guided movement gives the best chance of restoring opening
- Opening is not improving over a week or two
- Eating or cleaning your teeth is difficult
