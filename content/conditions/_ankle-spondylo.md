## blurb
Sometimes ongoing heel pain is driven by inflammation from a broader joint condition rather than overuse alone, especially if it keeps flaring up over a long time.

## noticed
- Heel pain that flares and settles repeatedly over months or years
- Pain that can affect the bottom or back of the heel and doesn't fully fit typical patterns
- Sometimes appears alongside other joint pain, back stiffness, or skin/eye symptoms

## homeCare
- Keep track of any other joint, back, skin, or eye symptoms to share with your healthcare provider
- Stay gently active and stretch as tolerated
- Follow guidance from your medical team on managing overall health

## seePhysioIf
- Heel pain keeps coming back over a long period, especially with other joint symptoms
- Usual heel pain treatments like stretching or orthotics aren't helping
