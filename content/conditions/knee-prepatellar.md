## blurb
A small fluid sac sits in front of the kneecap to let the skin glide over it. A lot of kneeling, or a knock, can irritate it so that it fills with fluid into a soft swelling on the front of the kneecap. It is common in people who kneel for work. A hot, red swelling, or feeling unwell, needs a doctor, as it may be infected.

## noticed
- A soft swelling on the front of the kneecap
- Tender to kneel on
- Often after a lot of kneeling, such as flooring, gardening, or cleaning

## homeCare
- Avoid kneeling on it; use thick knee pads if you must kneel
- A cold pack can ease the swelling
- Keep the knee moving normally

## seePhysioIf
- The swelling has not gone down after 2 to 3 weeks
- It keeps coming back with work
- It becomes hot, red, or you feel unwell: see a doctor the same day
