## blurb
A nerve wraps around the bone just below the outer knee, and a branch runs down the outer shin to the top of the foot. Crossing the legs for long periods, a tight cast or brace, or a knock can press on it, giving tingling or numbness on the outer shin and top of the foot. If the foot slaps down or the toes catch, a doctor should check it.

## noticed
- Tingling, burning, or numbness on the outer shin and top of the foot
- Worse after crossing the legs, a tight boot or brace, or a knock below the outer knee
- Sometimes a weak foot or toes that catch

## homeCare
- Avoid crossing your legs and pressure on the outer knee
- Loosen tight boots, braces, or straps
- Keep the ankle moving normally

## seePhysioIf
- The tingling has not eased after a few weeks
- Your foot slaps down or your toes catch: see a doctor first
- Moving your back changes it: the back may be the source
