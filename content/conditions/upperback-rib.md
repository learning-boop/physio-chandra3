## blurb
Each rib joins the spine at small joints that move with every breath. A twist, lift, cough or sneeze can strain one, giving a sharp spot beside the spine that can wrap along the rib towards the side.

## noticed
- A sharp spot beside the spine that catches with a deep breath, cough or sneeze
- Pain that follows the line of a rib towards the side
- Twisting or turning brings it on

## homeCare
- Keep breathing normally, and take a few slow deep breaths each hour so the rib keeps moving
- Gentle upper-body turns and side bends within comfort
- A heat pack over the sore spot, and hugging a pillow to support a cough or sneeze

## seePhysioIf
- It has not eased after a week or two
- Every breath or sleeping on that side stays painful
- It keeps returning with twisting at work or in sport
