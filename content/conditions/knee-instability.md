## blurb
This happens when the kneecap shifts partly or fully out of its groove, usually after a twisting movement, and it's most common in younger people.

## noticed
- A feeling that the kneecap has shifted, popped, or moved out to the side
- Sudden buckling or the knee giving way, especially when twisting or pivoting
- A nervous or unstable feeling when pressure is put on the kneecap from the side

## homeCare
- Avoid twisting or pivoting movements that trigger the unstable feeling
- Once things settle, gentle strengthening of the thigh and hip muscles can help
- Ease back into sport gradually and with controlled movements

## seePhysioIf
- Your kneecap has visibly moved out of place, even if it went back by itself
- You keep getting the feeling that your knee might give way
