## blurb
The tissue under the skin of the palm can slowly thicken into lumps and cords, gradually pulling a finger, most often the ring or little finger, down into the palm. It is usually painless and runs in families. Stretching does not stop it, but treatment can help once the hand no longer lies flat on a table.

## noticed
- Firm lumps or cords in the palm, usually not painful
- A finger slowly bending into the palm
- Not being able to lay the hand flat on a table

## homeCare
- Check whether your hand lies flat on a table every few months
- Avoid forcing the finger straight
- Use thicker grips if the lumps are tender

## seePhysioIf
- Your hand no longer lies flat on a table: a hand surgeon may be worth seeing
- The bend is getting in the way of everyday tasks
- You have had treatment and need splinting and exercises afterwards
