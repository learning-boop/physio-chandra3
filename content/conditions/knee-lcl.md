## blurb
This is a stretch or tear of the ligament on the outer side of your knee, typically from a force pushing the knee outward or a direct blow to the inner leg.

## noticed
- Pain and tenderness along the outer side of the knee after an injury
- Localized puffiness right over the outer knee
- A sense of looseness when the knee is pushed outward

## homeCare
- Rest from aggravating movements and apply ice in the first couple of days
- Try a supportive brace if extra stability feels reassuring
- Gradually rebuild movement and strength as symptoms ease

## seePhysioIf
- Symptoms aren't settling after a couple of weeks of rest and care
- The knee feels loose or shifts to the side during activity
