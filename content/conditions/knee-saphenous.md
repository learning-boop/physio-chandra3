## blurb
A skin nerve runs down the inside of the knee to the inner shin. It can be irritated by knee surgery, a knock, or pressure, giving a burning, tingling, or numb patch on the inner knee or shin. It is a feeling nerve only, so the leg is not weak.

## noticed
- A burning, tingling, or numb patch on the inner knee or shin
- Often after knee surgery or a knock
- No weakness in the leg

## homeCare
- Avoid pressure on the inner knee, such as crossing the legs or tight braces
- Keep the knee moving normally
- Gentle desensitising, such as rubbing the area with different textures

## seePhysioIf
- The burning has not eased after a few weeks
- The patch is spreading, or the leg feels weak: that points to the back instead
- It is limiting sleep or walking
