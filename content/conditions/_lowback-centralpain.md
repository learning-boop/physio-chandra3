## blurb
In some long-standing cases, the nervous system becomes extra sensitive, so pain can feel widespread or stronger than the original strain would suggest.

## noticed
- Pain that has lasted for several months and feels spread beyond just the low back
- Increased sensitivity to touch, movement, or activities that wouldn't normally bother you
- Pain that doesn't clearly match one single movement or position pattern

## homeCare
- Build general fitness gradually with low-intensity activities like walking or swimming
- Focus on steady, consistent activity rather than pushing through intense exercise
- Prioritize good sleep and stress management alongside gentle movement

## seePhysioIf
- Your pain feels widespread and has lasted more than three months
- You're finding it hard to stay active because the pain feels out of proportion to activity
