## blurb
Just in front of the bony bump on the outer ankle is a small soft hollow. After sprains, or with a foot that rolls in a lot, the tissue there can stay sore and the ankle can feel less steady, especially on uneven ground. It usually improves with strength, balance and footwear changes.

## noticed
- An ache in the soft hollow just in front of the outer ankle bone
- Worse on uneven ground, slopes or with running
- Often after one or more sprains, sometimes with a feeling the ankle could give way

## homeCare
- Supportive shoes, and an insole if your foot rolls in a lot
- Balance practice on one leg, and strengthening of the ankle and calf
- Ease off uneven ground for a while

## seePhysioIf
- It is not settling after 2 to 3 weeks
- The ankle keeps rolling or giving way
- The ankle also locks, or keeps swelling: see us, as a scan may be needed

## clinicNotes
- Lateral hindfoot pain over the sinus tarsi after inversion sprains or with excessive pronation; subtalar instability, interosseous or cervical ligament injury, synovitis. Tender sinus tarsi; pain on supination and on inversion with plantarflexion. Diagnostic anaesthetic injection or MRI if unclear. Management: orthoses, proprioceptive and peroneal strengthening, mobilisation; injection or arthroscopy if persistent.
