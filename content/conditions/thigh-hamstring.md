## blurb
This is an injury to the muscle group at the back of your thigh, usually happening during a sprint, kick, or sudden change of direction. It causes a sharp pain right at the moment of injury and can leave the area sore and weak for days to weeks afterward.

## noticed
- A sudden, sharp pain at the back of the thigh or right where the thigh meets the sit-bone, often during running or kicking
- Tenderness when pressing along the back of the thigh, and sometimes bruising or swelling
- Pain or tightness when trying to bend forward, stretch the leg straight, or push hard with that leg, plus a limp right after the injury

## homeCare
- Ease off sprinting, kicking, and hard running for the first few days, switching to gentle walking as comfort allows
- Apply ice and keep the leg gently moving within a pain-free range early on rather than staying completely still
- Gradually add gentle stretching and light strengthening (such as slow, controlled lowering exercises) over the following weeks, building back up to running and sport drills step by step

## seePhysioIf
- The pain isn't easing after a couple of weeks, or you're still limping when walking
- You've strained this area before and want a structured plan to get back to sport safely and lower the chance of it happening again
