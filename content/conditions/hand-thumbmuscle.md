## blurb
The small muscles that move the thumb and index finger work hard with texting, gaming, and heavy pinching. When overloaded they ache at the base of the thumb and along the index finger, and tight spots in them can send pain into the thumb. It usually settles as the load is changed and spread out.

## noticed
- An ache at the base of the thumb or along the side of the index finger
- Worse after long spells of texting, gaming, or pinching
- Tender, tight spots in the fleshy part of the thumb

## homeCare
- Take regular breaks from texting and gaming, and switch hands or use voice input
- Gently stretch the thumb away from the palm
- Massage the fleshy part of the thumb, and use warmth

## seePhysioIf
- The ache has not settled after 2 to 3 weeks of changes
- Pinching or gripping is getting weaker
- You want advice on how you hold your phone, tools, or controller
