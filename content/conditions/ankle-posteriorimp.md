## blurb
Deep at the back of the ankle, tissue or a small extra bone (the os trigonum) can be pinched when the foot points down hard, as in ballet on pointe, kicking, or going downhill. The pain is deep behind the ankle rather than at the heel cord.

## noticed
- A deep pain at the back of the ankle, in front of the heel cord
- Worse pointing the foot down hard: dance, kicking, downhill running
- Sometimes pain with bending the big toe

## homeCare
- Ease off the movements that point the foot down hard for a while
- Keep the ankle moving in the range that does not pinch
- Keep the calf and the rest of the leg strong

## seePhysioIf
- It keeps coming back with dance or sport
- The ankle also swells or catches
- It started after a sprain or a fall and is not settling: see us, as an X-ray may be needed

## clinicNotes
- Posterior ankle impingement: the forced plantarflexion test reproduces posterior pain; os trigonum or Stieda process on a lateral X-ray; FHL tenosynovitis (pain with resisted hallux flexion, triggering). Dancers, footballers, downhill runners. Management: load modification, technique, mobilisation, FHL loading; injection or arthroscopic excision if it fails.
- Sources: AIM Theory Manual 2023 Ch. 2.8; JOSPT ankle sprain CPG 2021 (os trigonum as copathology).
