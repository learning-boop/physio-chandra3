## blurb
A sudden push-off, such as a lunge in tennis or a sprint, can tear some fibres of the calf muscles. It often feels like being hit in the calf, is common in active people in middle age, and may bruise down towards the ankle. Because a clot in the calf can look similar, a swollen, warm, tender calf needs a doctor to check it.

## noticed
- A sudden sharp pain in the calf while pushing off, like being hit
- Pain walking or rising onto the toes
- Sometimes bruising that tracks down to the ankle

## homeCare
- Ease off running and jumping for the first days; keep walking as comfort allows
- A small heel raise in the shoe can ease walking early on
- Start gentle calf raises as it settles, building up gradually

## seePhysioIf
- You still cannot walk normally after a few days
- You want a plan to return to sport: re-injury is common
- The whole calf becomes swollen, warm, or tender: see a doctor the same day
