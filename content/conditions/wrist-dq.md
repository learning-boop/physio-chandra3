## blurb
The tendons that move the thumb run through a snug tunnel on the thumb side of the wrist. Repetitive gripping and lifting — classically a new baby, kettle, phone — can irritate that sheath.

## noticed
- Achy or sharp thumb-side wrist pain, sometimes up the forearm
- Worse with gripping, pinching, lifting, texting
- A tender 'strip' or slight swelling near the thumb base
- A painful catch with thumb movement

## homeCare
- Modify the provoking lift — scoop with the palm up rather than thumb-first
- A thumb-spica splint for aggravating tasks can calm it
- Gradual return to load as pain settles

## seePhysioIf
- Thumb-side pain lasts more than 1–2 weeks
- It returns whenever you resume normal activity
- Splints or rest alone haven't fixed it — guided loading usually does
