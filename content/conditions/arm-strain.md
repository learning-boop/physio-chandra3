## blurb
The big muscles of the upper arm can ache for a day or three after a new or harder workout, or be strained by a sudden heavy lift. Soreness that peaks after a day or two and then eases is normal and settles on its own; a sharp pain during a lift with bruising after is a strain, which usually heals over a few weeks with graded loading.

## noticed
- Aching and stiffness in the biceps or triceps 1 to 3 days after exercise
- A sharp pain during a lift, sometimes with bruising afterwards
- It stays in the muscle and is sore to press or to use

## homeCare
- Keep the arm gently moving; light activity eases soreness faster than full rest
- For a strain, ease off heavy lifting for a week or two, then build back gradually
- Heat and gentle stretching for comfort
- Increase training loads a little at a time rather than in big jumps

## seePhysioIf
- A strain has not improved after 2 weeks
- You felt a pop and the muscle looks a different shape
- You want a plan to return to lifting or sport
