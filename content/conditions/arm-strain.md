## blurb
The big muscles of the upper arm can ache for a day or three after a new or harder workout, or be strained by a sudden heavy lift. Soreness that peaks after a day or two and then eases is normal and settles on its own; a sharp pain during a lift with bruising after is a strain, which usually heals over a few weeks with graded loading.

## noticed
- Aching and stiffness in the biceps or triceps 1 to 3 days after exercise
- A sharp pain during a lift, sometimes with bruising afterwards
- It stays in the muscle and is sore to press or to use

## homeCare
- Keep the arm gently moving; light activity eases soreness faster than full rest
- For a strain, ease off heavy lifting for a week or two, then build back gradually
- Heat and gentle stretching for comfort
- Increase training loads a little at a time rather than in big jumps
- In the first 2 days, rest it a little and use a cold pack wrapped in a cloth for 10 to 15 minutes at a time; avoid hard stretching early on

## seePhysioIf
- A strain has not improved after 2 weeks
- You felt a pop and the muscle now looks a different shape, or bending or straightening the arm is weak: see a doctor the same day, as a tendon tear is best repaired early
- It is not getting better after 4 to 6 weeks: see your doctor
- You want a plan to return to lifting or sport

## physioHelps
<!-- Approved by Chandra Matla, 17 Oct 2026 (review: content/review/physioHelps-review-2026-10-17.docx) -->
- A physiotherapist can check whether it is normal soreness or a strain and explain what is going on.
- A graded strengthening plan can help the muscle heal and rebuild its strength.
- You get advice on building training loads up gradually to lower the chance of another strain.

## clinicNotes
- Strain grade I-III (Järvinen 2005; elbow document form 6, 19 Oct 2026): mechanism, bruising or contour defect, tenderness at the belly or musculotendinous junction, pain on resisted contraction and passive stretch. Rule out distal biceps (hook test) and triceps rupture. Brachialis and pronator teres strains: elbow-biceps, forearm-overuse. After a direct blow, avoid early deep massage and forced stretch (myositis ossificans). Graded loading (ACOEM 2013); PSFS.
