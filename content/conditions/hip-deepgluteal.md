## blurb
The sciatic nerve passes under the deep buttock muscles; tightness or overload there can irritate it, causing deep buttock pain and sometimes tingling into the thigh — a pattern that mimics low-back sciatica.

## noticed
- Deep, hard-to-point-at buttock pain
- Worse with prolonged sitting, especially hard surfaces
- Sometimes tingling into the back of the thigh
- Tender deep in the buttock muscles

## homeCare
- Break up long sitting; use a cushion on hard chairs
- Gentle figure-4 stretches and hip mobility
- Gradual gluteal strengthening

## seePhysioIf
- Buttock pain persists beyond 2 weeks
- Any leg tingling — the low back must be ruled out as the true source
- Sitting tolerance is limiting work or driving
