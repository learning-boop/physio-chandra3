## blurb
The tibialis posterior tendon holds up the arch from the inner ankle. When overloaded it aches behind the inner ankle bone and, over time, can let the arch flatten — early treatment matters.

## noticed
- Pain along the inner ankle/arch, behind the inner ankle bone
- A flatter arch or 'too many toes' look from behind
- Feet that tire quickly; difficulty or pain with single-leg heel raises

## homeCare
- Supportive footwear; temporary arch support can offload the tendon
- Avoid pushing through long walks that flare it
- Begin gentle heel-raise strengthening within comfort

## seePhysioIf
- Inner-ankle pain with any new arch flattening — early care shortens recovery
- Pain with stairs, hills, or longer standing
- A single-leg heel raise is painful or impossible
