## blurb
The small joint where the collarbone meets the shoulder blade can be sprained by a fall onto the shoulder or irritated by load (bench press, dips). Pain sits right on the bony point at the top. Mild sprains usually settle over a few weeks.

## noticed
- Pointable pain on the very top of the shoulder
- Worse reaching across the body or lying on it
- Sometimes a small, long-standing bump over the joint (from an old sprain)

## homeCare
- Reduce cross-body and heavy pressing movements for now
- Ice or heat over the point of pain for comfort
- A sling only briefly, if needed for comfort, then wean off it
- Keep the rest of the shoulder moving normally

## seePhysioIf
- A new step or bump at the top of the shoulder after a fall or blow, or a child under 16 with a painful shoulder after a fall: see a doctor or urgent care the same day for an X-ray before starting exercises
- Pain persists past about 2 to 3 weeks
- You want a graded return to gym or sport

## clinicNotes
- Kelley 2013 lists AC joint sprain and AC osteoarthritis among shoulder differentials; neither shoulder CPG gives AC test accuracy. Chronopoulos 2004: cross-body adduction sens 77%, spec 79%; all three of cross-body adduction, resisted extension and active compression positive, sens 25%, spec 97%. A painful arc at the top of the range (not mid-range) points away from the rotator cuff; deep or anterior pain and passive stiffness point to the glenohumeral joint.
- Grades (Rockwood): I-II managed without surgery (usually settle in 1-6 weeks); III debated (similar long-term function; about 22% of non-operative cases later have surgery; meta-analyses 2024-25); IV-VI surgical. A step deformity after injury needs bilateral AP or Zanca X-rays before rehabilitation. In children a fall onto the shoulder is more often a clavicle (physeal) fracture. Weightlifters: distal clavicle osteolysis.
- Sources: Kelley MJ et al., JOSPT 2013; Desmeules F et al., JOSPT 2025; Chronopoulos E et al., Am J Sports Med 2004; AC type III meta-analyses 2024-25 (PMC11590540; EOR 2025); Chandra's AC joint protocol.
