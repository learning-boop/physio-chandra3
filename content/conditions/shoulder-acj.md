## blurb
The small joint where the collarbone meets the shoulder blade can be sprained by a fall onto the shoulder or irritated by load (bench press, dips). Pain sits right on the bony point at the top.

## noticed
- Pointable pain on the very top of the shoulder
- Worse reaching across the body or lying on it
- Sometimes a small bump over the joint

## homeCare
- Reduce cross-body and heavy pressing movements for now
- Ice or heat over the point of pain for comfort
- Keep the rest of the shoulder moving normally

## seePhysioIf
- Pain persists past ~2–3 weeks
- There's a visible step or bump after an injury
- You want a graded return to gym or sport
